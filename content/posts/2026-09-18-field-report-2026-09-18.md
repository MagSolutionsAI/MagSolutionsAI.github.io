**Withdrawn — 27 September 2026.** This article was published automatically on 18 September 2026
with figures that were **not measured**. They came from the fixtures of our own test suite: a test
wrote a sample article into the file our server serves, a deployment shipped that file, and the
blog workflow published it without checking it against the live measurement.

Every figure it contained was fictitious: 1000 pull requests analysed, 1200 dependencies resolved,
900 distinct package names, 10 candidates reviewed by hand, 80 detector corrections across 20 cited
repositories, and the source `a/b#1`. None of them should be quoted. The report our server actually
generated from the measurement that day recorded **1,246** pull requests analysed, **1,583**
dependencies resolved and **0** hallucinated packages confirmed.

What we changed so it cannot happen again: the test suite now fails if it modifies any file that is
deployed; that file is no longer part of a deployment; and the blog refuses to publish a report
whose figures do not match [the live measurement](https://api.magsolutionsai.com/measurement).

The real, current figures are always at [api.magsolutionsai.com/measurement](https://api.magsolutionsai.com/measurement), and our record
of mistakes is at [How often we are wrong](https://magsolutionsai.com/quality.html).
