FAQ

Questions people actually ask before paying us.

▸What counts as a private repository?
Any repository on your GitHub organisation or account that isn't public. Public repositories are always free and unlimited, on every plan — including Free.
▸What happens if I go over my plan's repo limit?
Nothing breaks automatically. Audits keep running on every repository you've installed the app on. If your usage consistently exceeds your plan, we'll reach out to find the right tier together — we don't believe in surprise lockouts.
▸Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Subscriptions are billed monthly with no lock-in contract. Cancel from the payment receipt Stripe emails you, or email us and we'll do it for you.
▸Is my source code stored anywhere?
No. Diffs are analysed in memory and discarded. We store the installation ID, repository name, PR number and risk level — never your source, never a detected secret. Full detail in the Trust section.
▸How do I upgrade, downgrade or change my plan?
Right now: email us and we'll switch it within a business day. A self-serve billing portal is on the roadmap but not built yet — we'd rather tell you that than pretend it exists.
▸Do I need a credit card to try it?
No. Install the Free plan directly from GitHub — no card, no signup form, no trial clock. Upgrade only when you actually need private repositories covered.
